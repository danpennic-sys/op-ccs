## SEE Step (protocol boundary for this module)

SEE is the first forced action once a collapse threshold is crossed.

- Observe and record only.
- Capture observable metrics, constraints, and context hash.
- Produce an evidence artifact.
- No generation, no decision, no mutation, no continuation without a subsequent recorded step.

All later steps (SET, ACT, VERIFY, RECORD) are invalid unless a SEE evidence record exists in the chain.
